**Yes, you can use Pong League for any tournament where teams or single players compete against each other and a result comes out at the end.** Whether it is a console tournament, a football tournament, a table football round or a company party: you set up the tournament, the group stage with tables and schedule is created automatically, and then you play out the winner in a knockout bracket. The name says beer pong, but the tournament logic behind it is not tied to one sport.

## What can a tournament in Pong League do?

This applies to every tournament, no matter what you play:

- **2 to 64 teams** and any number of groups. Teams are spread evenly across the groups, and you can redraw with "Shuffle" if you like.
- **Schedule:** In each group, everyone plays everyone once. Pong League creates the plan for you.
- **Live tables:** Games, wins, losses, goals scored, difference, points and the form of the last games.
- **Invite by link and QR code:** Teams join themselves, so you do not have to enter everyone one by one.
- **Correct results at any time:** Mistyped something? A result can be edited later and the table recalculates.
- **Knockout phase with a bracket** for up to 32 teams (part of Pro, a one-time unlock without a subscription).

It runs in the browser, no installation needed. If you only want to follow the tournament, the link is enough.

## How to organize a console tournament: EA Sports FC, Mario Kart and more

Console tournaments are a perfect fit: one evening, many players, one TV. Whether it is **EA Sports FC** (formerly FIFA), **Mario Kart**, **Rocket League** or a fighting game: you enter the players or two-person teams as teams, and Pong League does the maths.

1. **Create the tournament:** Set the name, number of teams and groups. With 8 players, two groups of 4 work well.
2. **Enter the players:** The team name is simply the player name or the team name you compete under.
3. **Play the group stage:** After each match, you enter the result, for example the goals or race points. The table updates immediately.
4. **Play the final:** The best of each group play out the winner in the knockout bracket.

That keeps the overview intact on a console night, even when friends come and go.

## How to plan a football tournament: schedule, table and knockout round

For an indoor tournament, a small-sided tournament, a club festival or a company tournament, the automatic schedule pays off. You add the teams, spread them across groups and get the complete plan for the group stage.

![Pong League with a football tournament: table of Group A with goals, difference and points, plus the "Next match" card](/images/news/turnier-fussball-beispiel.webp)

This is how the tables are calculated:

- **3 points for a win, 1 point for a draw, 0 for a loss.**
- The ranking comes from **points**, then the **difference** in goals, then the **wins**.
- The "Treffer" (hits) column shows goals scored and conceded, for example 10:4 in the example picture.

There are no draws in the knockout bracket, with one game per pairing. If a game there is decided after extra time or a penalty shootout, enter the final result.

## More tournaments you can plan with Pong League

Anything where teams or players compete with a result works:

- **Table football tournament** at the office or club
- **Darts, table tennis or pool tournaments**
- **Volleyball, Spikeball or cornhole** in the garden
- **Flunkyball championships** and **beer Olympics** with several disciplines, each as its own tournament
- **Company tournaments** or **club festivals** with many teams
- **Casual leagues among friends**

Pong League accepts every result as a number. Whether that is goals, cups, points or legs is up to you.

## What Pong League cannot do

So you are not disappointed: Pong League is a tournament manager, not sports software. There are, for example, no top scorer lists, no cards and no match times. One result is recorded per game, and in the group stage everyone plays everyone. Up to four players per team can link their player profile. For larger squads, simply enter the team name.
