We are working on something many of you have asked for: **BeerPong Sports Tec is becoming an app.** It will be available soon on Google Play and in the Apple App Store.

## Why a dedicated app?

You do not play beer pong at a desk, but at the table with your phone in your hand. That is why we are building the app from the ground up for smartphones instead of just wrapping the website. It should feel fast and be easy to use with a few taps during a game.

## What to expect

- **Pong Stats and Pong League in one app:** Record throws and matches, look at your statistics and organize tournaments, all in one place.
- **Made for your phone:** Large touch areas, clear team colors and a design that stays easy to read in a dark bar.
- **No detours:** You open the app and you are right in the action. You no longer need the website's landing page for that.

## When does it launch?

We cannot give an exact date yet. We will launch on Google Play first, followed by the Apple App Store. Both stores review new apps before they go live, so we do not want to promise a date we cannot be sure to keep.

## What about the website?

The web app stays as it is, and you can keep using it as usual.

## Stay up to date

As soon as the app is available, you will read it here in the news. Feel free to check back regularly. Until then, our guide on [how to plan a beer pong tournament](/en/news/beer-pong-turnier-ideen) shows you how to make tournaments exciting. That already works in the web app today.
